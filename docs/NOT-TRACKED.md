# Not tracked yet

Things the app shows as a description only, or tracks partly. Kept up to date as rounds land; strike items when they become tracked.

## Engine limits (affect several features)
- **Speeds that depend on a choice after a rest** (Path of the Beast's Bestial Soul) or on a form (Circle of Stars' Dragon) aren't tracked; other fly, swim and climb speeds from features and spells (Fly, Spider Climb, Gaseous Form) are.
- **Feature timers** come from the duration a use states ("for 1 minute"); a feature whose wording doesn't say it gets none.

## Per class (summary only, or partly tracked)
- **Barbarian**: Totem Warrior Wolf spirit/aspect, Bear and Eagle attunement; Shielding Storm; Warrior of the Gods.
- **Bard**: Blade Flourish +10 ft speed; Magical Inspiration and Bardic Versatility (summary).
- **Cleric**: Blessed Strikes, Improved Flare, Thunderbolt Strike, Stormborn, Improved Duplicity, Order's Wrath; Several conditional bonuses are tick-boxes, not automatic (Soul of the Forge, Avatar of Battle, Saint of Forge and Fire).
- **Druid**: Moon druid features rely on the app's Wild Shape handling; Twinkling Constellations, Fungal Body, Primal Strike.
- **Fighter**: Arcane Shot effects and their 18th-level increase; Cavalier/Samurai "or a language" alternatives; Samurai's alternate save; Know Your Enemy, Relentless, Weapon Bond's no-disarm, Rapid Strike; Martial Versatility.
- **Monk**: Four Elements discipline effects; Astral Self's Wisdom-for-attacks and three-attack rule; Kensei's one-melee-one-ranged rule; Dedicated Weapon.
- **Paladin**: Aura of Conquest and Scornful Rebuke (damage to others), Invincible Conqueror's extra attack (its resistances and crit range are tracked), Mortal Bulwark's truesight, Living Legend/Mortal Bulwark slot refuel, Aura of Alacrity and Aura of the Sentinel (only your own bonus).
- **Ranger**: Mighty Swarm (built into Gathered Swarm's die).
- **Rogue**: Infiltration Expertise, Impostor, Magical Ambush, Insightful Manipulator, Soul of Deceit, Fancy Footwork, Death's Friend; Whispers of the Dead offers skills only (no tools).
- **Sorcerer**: Unearthly Recovery's half-HP heal; Additional Sorcerer Spells, Sorcerous Versatility.
- **Warlock**: Gift of the Sea swim speed, Elemental Gift flight and its genie-kind resistance, Awakened Mind telepathy, Beguiling Defenses' charm immunity, Genie's Wrath damage type.
- **Wizard**: Arcane Ward is tracked as full after each long rest even before it's created; Minor Alchemy, Split Enchantment, Alter Memories, Malleable Illusions, Master of Hexes, Sanctuary Vessel; Transmuter's Stone benefit; Grim Harvest healing (note); Durable Magic uses a switch you turn on while concentrating.

## Feats
- Maneuvers: the app adds the die and spends it; what the maneuver does to the target (prone, pushed, frightened) is text with its DC. Sweeping Attack's second target, Commander's Strike's ally attack, Evasive Footwork's AC while moving and Parry's reduction are shown, not applied.
- One maneuver per attack is a warning, not a block.
- Smites: the next-hit tag ends when its damage is rolled (Thunderous Smite's concentration too; the others keep concentration for their lasting part); the spell's lasting part (Branding's light, Blinding's blindness, Staggering's save) is text.
- Elemental Adept's minimum per die is applied when the app rolls; with your own dice, count low dice up yourself (the roll says so).
- Healer's once-per-creature limit resets when you rest (the app doesn't know when others rest).
- Ritual Caster's rituals can be cast any way the app allows; cast them as rituals.
- Heavy Armor Master needs you to mark magical damage (the switch shows on the damage pad when it applies).

## Your own actions and spells
- A custom attack uses the Attack action or a bonus action; a reaction attack is a "use" with its note, rolled from the Actions list like any attack of that kind.
- A custom spell needs a spell list to cast with; a character with no spellcasting gets no attack bonus or DC for it.
- Conditions a custom action imposes are named in its note; put them on the target yourself.

## Races (Volo's)
- Triton's Wall of Water isn't in the spell content (it's an XGtE spell); cast it from the book.
- Long-Limbed reach, Powerful Build, Surprise Attack's once per combat and Feline Agility's recharge are reminders, not tracked.

## Backstory and calendars
- The Μηθειολόγιο copy has the months, week, seasons and era from the table's Fantasy Calendar; moons, events and named years aren't shown, and the app doesn't sync with the website (set the date by hand if it moves on there).

## Auras and tags
- Aura effects from allies (Add an effect → Other) don't know whether you're inside the aura or whether its owner is conscious: remove them yourself.
- Twilight Sanctuary's per-turn temporary hit points aren't rolled for other creatures; the reminder says what to do.
- Feature DCs are listed for the features that need one (Stunning Strike, Turn Undead, maneuvers, Breath Weapon, ...); a feature missing from that list shows no DC.

## Summoned creatures and forms
- Grappled/restrained speeds etc. come from effects only; "lethargy"-style effects on summons aren't automatic.
- Creature spell slots for creatures without a Spellcasting trait are free-form ("cast anyway").

## Other
- Barbarian override in the table pack only covers levels up to 9 explicitly (higher levels come from the SRD).
- Weapon-specific Shillelagh: offered on every melee weapon, flagged when it isn't a club or quarterstaff.

## Effects
- Effects are drawn where you last tapped (spells, Inspiration) or at the roll's total / HP box / concentration chip; there's no per-spell-row anchor.
- Damage prevention shows for immunity, resistance, Heavy Armor Master, temp HP and Durable; a reduction rolled by hand before entering the damage (Parry, Deflect Missiles' panel) is already subtracted and doesn't show a shield.
- No sound. Vibration on Android only.
- 3D dice: only rolls made in the roll panel are thrown; quick rolls the app makes by itself (a feature's temp HP, starting gold) stay digital. A d3 or d100 in a roll is rolled by the app. The default dice show 6 and 9 alike when upside down. Reduced motion doesn't turn 3D dice off (it's your explicit choice).
- Damage types: a roll with several types plays only the main one (the most damage). Typed damage you take plays only when all of it got through; when some was stopped, the shield ring plays. Necrotic Shroud's frightening and Form of Dread's frightening hit are on other creatures, so they have no effect here.
- Nature spells: where the area is isn't recorded, so the looks grow toward where you tapped. Grasping Vine's later lashes (bonus action, no damage) have no effect. Wall of Thorns' wall isn't placed or shaped (line or ring): it always grows across the ground.
- Spell effects: Moonbeam's position and movement and Gust of Wind's direction aren't recorded, so their effects can't follow them. Control Water's or Weather's mode changed later (a new action) isn't recorded: change the tag's choice by hand. Hand of Harm in a Flurry at 11th (no ki) shows no effect.

## Spells with two damage types
- Flame Strike: casting it higher adds the extra 1d6 to the fire; the spell lets you put it on the radiant instead (add it by hand if you do).
- Spell lists exported one class at a time give their class only when the file name says whose list it is ("spells_available_to_druids…"); a renamed file loses that.

## Items and scrolls
- Spell scrolls: the optional Scroll Mishap table is text only. A spell you write yourself for a scroll also joins your own spells.
- Spells given beyond the rules can be cast without a slot as often as you like: the app counts, the DM decides how many.

## Combat menus
- War Caster knows which spells can target only one creature for the SRD spells and the table's placeholders; a spell that exists only in your book file isn't offered (cast it from the Spells tab).
- Long-press was checked in a desktop browser with mouse and simulated touch; not yet on a real iPhone or Android phone.
- Healing Hands on someone else: the app spends the use and says how much; the other character's HP is changed on their own sheet.

## Expanded lists and custom effects
- Tasha's expanded lists are names-only data in the table pack; a spell the app doesn't have yet (not SRD, not in your book files) can't appear until a book file adds it. The table's ranger has no separate “Additional Ranger Spells” switch. Primal Awareness's spells are free once per long rest each; casting them with a slot works as usual.
- A custom spell's effect is applied when it's on you (cast with “Put its effect on me”); on another creature it's a reminder for them.
- The custom effect's description and a custom spell's reminder are shown, not applied.

## Custom items and notes
- Your own items can't be weapons or armor (no attack, no armor class from wearing it): for an attack, make your own action; for AC, add an Armor Class change to the item.
- An item's description is shown, not applied; only the changes you add are.
- Markdown: no tables, images or underline in the Backstory and notes; a list inside a list needs two spaces in front.
- A note written before in-world dates were kept is placed by its date text; if the table changed calendars since, it goes under “No in-world date” (its date text still shows).

## Your own companions
- One stat block each (no forms to switch between). Skills, damage resistances and condition immunities from a stat block go in its traits as text; checks use the ability modifier.
- Your own companions don't get command buttons (unless bonded as your Ranger's Companion): they act on their own turn or as your DM says. Spending your action or bonus action to command one is up to you (the combat pills).
- Ranger's Companion (bonded): the proficiency bonus goes on its AC, attacks, damage and the saves its stat block lists; its skill proficiencies aren't tracked (checks use the ability modifier). From 5th level, commanding it to attack and still making one weapon attack yourself is up to you; Exceptional Training's bonus-action command, Bestial Fury's second attack and Share Spells are text. A new beast after 8 hours: change the stat block in the builder. Only one should be bonded; the sheet warns if more are.
- An attack's note (a save, knocking prone) is shown, not applied.

## Ranger (Tasha's or PHB)
- Deft Explorer: Tireless's temporary hit points have no minimum of 1 applied (only with a negative Wisdom modifier would it matter); removing exhaustion on a short rest is text. Nature's Veil switches a "Nature's Veil" state on until the end of your next turn; being invisible is up to you (add the Invisible condition if your table wants its effects).
- Martial Versatility and Cantrip/Fighting Style swaps at an Ability Score Improvement: change the pick on the Sheet.
- Natural Explorer and Favored Enemy (PHB) keep their SRD text; their benefits (terrain travel, tracking, languages) aren't applied as numbers.

## Drakewarden
- The drake's Hit Dice (d10s equal to your ranger level) aren't tracked; summoning it again brings it back at full hit points.
- Infused Strikes (its reaction: +1d6 of its essence on an ally's weapon hit) and Drake Mount (riding it, no flying while ridden until 15th) are shown as traits, not rolled or applied.
- Drake's Breath rolls its damage when used and shows your spell save DC; the type you pick is named on the use, and the targets' saves are up to the table. Spending a 3rd-level or higher slot for another use: spend it on the slot pills.
- Reflexive Resistance counts its uses; halving the damage is up to you (or apply it as resistance when you enter the damage).
- Your resistance to the drake's essence lasts while it's summoned with hit points left; if it's dismissed some other way, summoning state isn't tracked beyond its hit points.

## Pact Magic with another class
- Getting slots back (Arcane Recovery, Natural Recovery, Harness Divine Power) restores your Spellcasting slots only, not Pact slots; use the slot pills by hand if your table allows otherwise.
- Font of Magic makes ordinary slots only (never Pact slots).

## 3D dice colours
- Colours are for the 3D dice only; "My dice" and "App rolls" look as before.
- Dice of a damage type without a colour of its own (none today) and dice of a size the 3D set doesn't have (d2, d3, d100: rolled by the app) aren't coloured.
- A reroll (Great Weapon Fighting) takes the colour of the first thrown die of that size, so with two groups of the same size it may show the other group's colour; the numbers are right either way.

## Dropping to 0 HP

- Strength of the Grave: the app can't tell whether the hit was a critical (damage is typed in), so it asks. "Damage taken" for the DC is the damage after resistance and vulnerability, temporary HP included. Closing the question without answering records no damage (as with Relentless Endurance): enter it again.
- Relentless Endurance and Death Ward work on damage you enter for yourself. Death Ward's other part (it ends one instant-death effect that isn't damage, like a Power Word Kill) is up to you: remove the Death Ward tag when it's used that way.
- Damage taken at 0 HP adds death save failures as before; neither feature applies then.

## Hound of Ill Omen and sorcery points
- The hound's target isn't tracked: its disadvantage on saves against your spells while the hound is within 5 ft is shown in its note, and you end the hound from its sheet when the target drops to 0.
- A hound set up by hand before this change (a form picked on its sheet) now shows only while the feature's timer runs.
- Elemental Affinity's Charisma bonus to one damage roll of your ancestry's type is offered as text; seeing through your own Darkness (Eyes of the Dark) is text.
- A second identical tap within about a second is ignored with a message; a real second use a moment later goes through (and only warns when you're out).

## Duplicate picks
- Overlaps between two fixed grants (a race and a background both giving the same skill) aren't offered as a new choice; the Sheet warns only when one of the two was your pick. Spells you already know aren't filtered from spell choices.

## Rituals and long castings

- Only spells are timed. Features or items that take minutes or hours to use (attuning, a short rest's activities, crafting) aren't.
- Holding concentration through a long casting (the 2014 rule for spells cast over more than one action) isn't modelled: starting a long casting doesn't end your other concentration; finishing it starts the new one as usual.
- One action under way at a time; starting another replaces it (the app says so).
- In combat it warns how many rounds the casting is, but doesn't stop the encounter or advance it round by round.
- War Caster and readied spells are unaffected (they're cast as a reaction, never timed).

## War Caster and book spells

- Whether a spell from your book text is aimed at one creature is read from its wording, conservatively: a few single-target spells are missed (ones that only name "a target", or have no attack, save or damage, like Power Word Kill) and a rare one is offered that arguably shouldn't be. The War Caster list is a suggestion; nothing stops you casting another spell.

## Backup reminder

- The reminder and the "last backup" date live in this browser's storage: a different browser or a cleared one starts over. It counts recorded changes only; importing a character or changing book text alone doesn't make a backup due.

## Custom effects

- A skill change applies to that skill's checks (and passive, for Perception, Investigation and Insight). Checks made with a different ability than usual (a Strength (Intimidation) check, as the DM may call for) aren't covered.

## Polymorph, True Polymorph and Shapechange

- Cast on another creature, nothing changes on your sheet (its statistics are the DM's). True Polymorph into an object, and its permanence after a full hour, aren't tracked: remove the effect when it's dispelled.
- Shapechange: your class features and spells while in a form are up to the form's body (the app reminds you); equipment falling, merging or being worn isn't tracked. Animal Shapes (on others) isn't tracked.

## Sending

- The reply isn't asked for: add it to the note (Edit) if you want it kept. A Sending from a scroll or an item goes through the same box; a readied Sending released later doesn't open it.

## Companions in combat

- A companion's turn is tracked by tapping its Action, Bonus, Reaction and Move: rolling one of its attacks doesn't mark its action by itself yet.
- With your own dice, companions' and summoned creatures' initiatives wait for you (the turn order shows "?"); the app rolls them only when it rolls your dice.
- A companion with its own initiative gets its turn back when you swipe "new turn" on it; the app doesn't step through the turn order for you.
- A mount you control moves on your turn: set your own mount to "On my turn" in the Companion Builder. Find Steed (a summon) already shares your turn.

## 3D dice

- The dice library draws in its own worker: effects can't follow a single die or fire on a bounce (the result strip and screen effects are drawn over it). Themes change a die's colour and material, not its shape. A natural 20's flames and a natural 1's ash play on its value in the strip and across the screen, not on the 3D die itself.
- Skip lets the app roll at once (the dice in the air don't count); Fast makes the dice heavier, so they settle in about a second.
- On this sandbox's software graphics, a throw takes about 3–6 s; a phone's GPU should be faster, but it hasn't been measured on a phone yet.
- Still rolled by the app (no 3D dice): Precision Attack's die added after the attack roll, and Recharge rolls (they happen on their own at the start of a turn). Metamagic rolls no dice of its own (Empowered Spell's rerolls aren't modelled), so it has no dice theme.
- Dice settings are per device, not per character or synced: a second phone starts from the defaults. Your own dice take a material from a short list (no editor for new ones); a homebrew material needs a theme folder added to the app.
- One hand or two is chosen on the roll (the app doesn't track what's in each hand); the choice is remembered per weapon on this device. Great Weapon Fighting follows it and rerolls every damage die of the attack showing 1 or 2, smite and maneuver dice included (table ruling). Dueling's "no other weapons" isn't checked.
- A creature attack's save damage is rolled with the hit; the app says what a success leaves, but the target's save (the DM's roll) isn't recorded. Extra damage is read from the creature's stat block: an attack you add or rename in the Companion Builder has only the damage you give it. Effects that come later (a disease, a wound at the start of each turn) stay in the attack's text.
- Effects on companions change their numbers (rolls, AC, speed, hit point maximum, resistances); what an effect does beyond numbers is on its chip only: Haste's extra action and the lethargy when it ends, a condition's "can't take actions". A spell's effect goes on a companion when you cast it from its panel (not a ritual or a casting of a minute or more). The companion's speed line under its stats doesn't change (its Move counter does).
- Jumping: the distances are shown; the movement a jump costs is entered with the movement buttons. Step of the Wind's doubled jump lasts until your next turn starts (the rule says for the turn). Bestial Soul's d10 is a note to roll. Boots of Striding and Springing's "walking speed at least 30 ft" isn't applied.
- Summoned spirits: their attack and DC are your spellcasting's when you cast (a later change doesn't update one already summoned). Once-a-day actions (Healing Touch, Dreadful Scream) and auras are in their text; the Draconic Spirit's shared resistance goes on you as "Shared Resistances" when you cast it (you pick the type). Summon Lesser Demons: roll the d6 yourself and pick the matching choice; the demons' hostility, the Charisma saves of a greater demon and the devil's talisman are in the spell's note.
- Dice performance has only been measured on a computer's software graphics, where throws render slowly; a real phone run is still needed. The app keeps the main thread about 9% busy while idle, dice or not (not yet looked into).
- A custom action made in the app can't pick a dice theme yet (content and homebrew packs can, with `diceTheme`).

- The seven spells carry their mechanics and class lists; their full text, the races' and feats' text and the 85 bestiary creatures come from your own book files (Characters → Book text), loaded on your device only.
- Nathair's Mischief: its card has a Mischievous Surge button (the d4 and what it means); pressing it at the start of each turn is up to you, the card reminds you. Ashardalon's Stride's fire damage and Draconic Transformation's breath are rolled from the spell or the effect's button; who they hit is yours to apply.
- Fizban's Platinum Shield on you adds half cover (+2 AC and Dexterity saves) and the resistances; evasion is a reminder. Moving it to someone else is ending it on you.
- Dragonborn (Fizban's): the breath weapon counts as one attack of the Attack action (the app warns past your number of attacks). Metallic Breath Weapon: pick Enervating or Repulsion when used; the targets' saves are the DM's.
- Gift of the Chromatic Dragon: Chromatic Infusion's +1d4 of the picked type is ticked on weapon damage for its minute (untick it for a weapon you didn't touch). Gift of the Gem Dragon: Telekinetic Reprisal's DC uses the ability the feat raised.

## Healing and effects on others (round 9)

- Who a spell can target is known for the common healing and helping spells (SPELL_TARGETS); for the rest the app offers every creature without a warning. Warnings never block.
- A spell's effect on a companion or a summoned creature ends with your concentration (a creature summoned by a concentration spell goes with it anyway).
- Features: Healing Hands, Lay on Hands and Bardic Inspiration pick who they go to. Lay on Hands spends the points you type even when the creature needs fewer (its HP is capped at its maximum). Curing a disease or poison with Lay on Hands is spent as points only.
- Potions: each applies its effect to whoever drinks it; "Someone else" isn't tracked (the potion is gone from your inventory). Diminution's weapons deal 1d4 less (subtracted in the roll, at least 1 damage; with your own dice, type the total with the small die taken away). Gaseous Form's resistance and limits, Water Breathing, Mind Reading, Clairvoyance, Animal Friendship and the Philter are reminders. Longevity: roll its effect on your age with your DM. Dragon's Majesty changes only your own form (given to a companion, the DM runs it); gem dragons appear once your bestiary file is loaded.

## Rune Knight (round 10)

- Each rune you know is a tag on Play, on by default; switch it off when its object isn't with you (its benefits and its damage invoke stop; its reaction and bonus-action buttons stay). Hill and Storm before fighter 7th: warned, kept.
- The Fire Rune's shackles: the target's save and its restraint are the DM's; the 2d6 at the start of its turns is a button to roll. Cloud and Stone are reactions with their text (and Stone's DC); Storm's advantage or disadvantage is given by you, applied by the DM.

## Racial and class traits (round 11a)

- Lucky, Brave, Fey Ancestry, Gnome Cunning: offered on every roll of their kind; whether the save is against fear, charm or magic is yours to say (Lucky is on by default and only changes a 1). Halfling Nimbleness (moving through larger creatures) is text.
- Stonecunning and Artificer's Lore: offered on History checks; whether the check is about stonework or a magic item is yours to say.
- Evasion: a note on Dexterity saves; the half-or-nothing damage is applied by you when you take it.
- Diamond Soul's reroll: an action that spends 1 ki; roll the save again from the Sheet.
- PHB Dragonborn Breath Weapon: the targets' saves and damage are the DM's; the app rolls the damage, shows the DC, and spends the use.

## Uses and recharges (round 11b)

- Initiative regains (Relentless, Ever-Ready Shot, Tireless Spirit, Superior Inspiration, Perfect Self): applied the first time you roll initiative in a combat; rolling it again in the same combat doesn't give another.
- Arcane Recovery, Natural Recovery: "after a short rest" is yours to say; the slots you pick come back.
- Combat Wild Shape: the healing goes to your beast form; the slot is spent even if you aren't transformed (noted).
- Stroke of Luck: ticked on a check before rolling, the d20 counts as 20; for an attack, the "miss hits" button spends it and you roll the damage.
- Dark One's Own Luck: ticked before the roll (the rules let you decide after seeing it; tick it and roll again if you forgot).
- Relentless Rage: each save counts as a use, success or failure; the count resets on a short or long rest.
- Song of Rest: your own extra die is a tick-box when you spend a Hit Die (once per rest is yours to keep); for others, the button rolls it for them.
- Fiendish Resilience: pick it after a rest from Actions; a tag "Fiendish Resilience: <type>" shows the resistance until the next pick replaces it. That magical and silvered weapons ignore it is yours to apply.
- Spell Mastery and Signature Spells: a pick that isn't in your spellbook is warned about (kept); whether Spell Mastery's spells are prepared isn't checked.
- Mighty Summoner: the extra hit points show as a change to the creature's maximum; that its natural weapons are magical is a note.
- Durable Summons: only for spells of the conjuration school.

## Damage and attack options (round 11c)

- Frenzy: switch it on when you rage (Actions → Frenzy); its bonus-action attack is rolled from your weapons. The exhaustion comes when Rage switches off or its timer ends.
- Overchannel: ticking it warns with this time's backlash; after the roll the app rolls the necrotic damage (or takes your dice's total) and asks before taking it (no type, so nothing reduces it; temporary HP still go first). A spell not cast as a wizard spell, or above 5th level, is warned about.
- Empowered Evocation: a second use in the same casting (another Scorching Ray ray, rolling the damage again) is warned about; Magic Missile's bonus is multiplied by its darts (its one roll covers them all). A spell not cast as a wizard spell is warned about. A spell with two damage rolls is rolled as one here, so the bonus counts once.
- Potent Cantrip, Magic Arrow, Primal Strike, Order's Wrath: notes on the rolls (magical damage isn't sent anywhere by the app).
- Combat Inspiration: the AC use is a reminder on the Valor die's tag.
- Rapid Strike: whether you had advantage is yours to say.
- Retaliation, Scornful Rebuke, Hurl Through Hell, Dark One's Blessing: buttons you press when their trigger happens.
- Holy Nimbus' 10 radiant damage to enemies and Draconic Presence's saves are the DM's.
- Wolf Spirit, Bear Attunement, Shielding Storm: tags while raging; your allies' benefits are theirs to track (Shielding Storm is in + Effect → Other).
- Fungal Body: the condition immunities are applied; a critical hit counting as normal is yours to apply when you enter the damage.
- Rage Beyond Death: at 0 HP while raging you aren't unconscious; when the rage ends there, the app marks you dead.
- Starry Form: the constellation is a switch; an older Starry Form in use before this update has no constellation switch (use Change constellation).

## Spell tags (round 11d)

- Spells on someone else: a timer tag reminds you it's running; its effect is theirs to track (they add it from + Effect).
- Spells that last until dispelled: a tag with no timer; remove it when it ends.
- Beacon of Hope: healing you receive being the maximum is yours to enter.
- Sanctuary: casting any spell asks whether it ends it (only spells that affect an enemy do).
- Wind Walk: changing form takes a minute (pick the form on its tag); the resistance in cloud form is yours to apply.
- Ray of Enfeeblement: halving the damage of Strength weapon attacks is yours (a note on the damage roll). Its saves, and Blindness/Deafness', are at the end of your turns.
- Animal Shapes: the beast's size limit and CR are checked (warned, not blocked); casting on others leaves their forms to them.

## Armor and encumbrance (round 11e-1)

- Stealth: disadvantage from the armor you wear (Medium Armor Master lifts it for medium armor).
- Heavy armor's Strength: 10 ft slower below it; dwarves aren't slowed.
- Encumbrance, by the checkbox on the Items page: carrying capacity (Strength × 15; over it is warned: beyond it you can only push, drag or lift) or the variant (speed and disadvantage applied). Size counts: Tiny half, Large twice, Huge four times, Gargantuan eight times; Powerful Build and Enlarge one size larger, Reduce one smaller. Every item in the inventory counts, even one kept in a Bag of Holding: the app doesn't know what's inside what.
- Beacon of Hope: someone else healing you is entered by hand (enter their maximum); the dice on screen still tumble, and each one counts as its highest face.
- Guardian of Faith: after each creature's save, tap "Failed the save" or "Saved"; the damage left counts down and the guardian vanishes at 60. Who is hostile and comes within 10 ft is yours to say.

## Ammunition and weapons (round 11e-2)

- Ammunition is counted only when "Track my ammunition" is ticked, and only when you say yes after an attack roll (a re-roll of the same attack asks again).
- Picking it up after a fight: half is offered; any number can be recorded.
- Using a weapon that needs ammunition as a melee weapon (an improvised weapon) isn't tracked.
- Long range: tick it when the target is beyond the normal range; the app doesn't know distances. Loading and Reach are notes.
- Silvered ammunition is noted on the roll; the DM decides what it overcomes.

## Charges, curses and artifacts (round 11e-3)

- Charges: use them on the item (or its action); the dawn roll is a button on the item, reminded after a long rest. Items without a charges entry in the content (most book items) are tracked as your own items with charges.
- Curses: what a curse makes you do (Sword of Vengeance's and Berserker Axe's Wisdom saves, Shield of Missile Attraction drawing attacks) is the DM's; the disadvantages that can be ticked are offered on the rolls. Only the items listed above (and your own items with a curse) have one in the app.
- Artifacts: properties with no numbers (a smell, a quest, a hostile spirit, the alignment rolled each dawn) are on the item's text. The ability score increase isn't capped at 24 by the app. A cast-from-the-artifact spell is once per dawn; roll the d6 yourself (on a 6, give the use back on its resource).


## Round 13a

- Circle of the Land: the land is picked in Choices; a Land druid made before this has no circle spells until it's picked (the Choices count shows it). Underdark is offered (PHB).
- Warlock patron lists: a book spell whose name differs from the SRD's (Tasha's Hideous Laughter / Hideous Laughter) is listed under both names. The Genie's kind is picked on its expanded list, and Genie's Wrath takes its damage type from it (no kind picked: no Genie's Wrath offered). The kind's Elemental Gift resistance comes from 6th level.
- Channel Divinity from two classes: one pool with the larger number of uses; it resets on a short rest as both classes' do.
- Primal Champion: a Strength or Constitution above 24 (from items or by hand) is warned, as any score above its maximum.

## Play page (round 13a-2)

- Hit Dice and Inspiration: hold a die about half a second; a tap, a swipe or scrolling does nothing. Keyboard: Enter or the arrow keys.
- Features: a use with several options (Channel Divinity) lists them; Harness Divine Power's own uses open it with the slot to pick; a resource spent only by ticking extra damage (Fire Rune: invoke) can be rolled from Play or ticked on the hit. A resource with its own die that nothing else spends (superiority dice spent by hand) keeps its die panel with "Just spend one".

## Capstones and immunities (round 13b)

- Archdruid: Wild Shape never runs out; ignoring spell components is yours to apply.
- Persistent Rage: the end-of-turn reminder changes; the 1-minute limit stays.
- Indomitable Might: on Strength checks (Athletics included) the total is raised to your Strength score, with the app's dice or yours.
- Mindless Rage: charmed and frightened immunity while raging; one already on you stays on its tag (suspended by the rules: ignore it until the rage ends).
- Nature's Ward: the charm and fright protection against elementals and fey is a note on saves.
- Disease immunity is listed with your immunities; diseases themselves aren't tracked.
- Stillness of Mind ends one charmed or frightened tag on you (the one you pick).
- Cleansing Touch: ending a spell on you is removing its tag; on someone else, it's theirs.
- Divine Intervention: from 20th level the action has no roll. After a success the 7-day wait is written in the Backstory (13c); the app doesn't stop you using it sooner.
- Foe Slayer: offered on every weapon attack and damage roll; whether the target is a favored enemy, and once per turn, are yours to keep.
- Thief's Reflexes: the second turn's initiative is a note; the turn order isn't changed.
- Empty Body: invisible (advantage on your attacks, a reminder about attacks against you) and resistance to all but force while it's on; Astral Projection for 8 ki is cast from the Spells tab.

## Uses, reminders and feature mechanics (round 13c)

- Once every N days (Divine Intervention after a success, Charm of Sunlight): a Backstory note says when it's back; nothing stops an earlier use.
- Mirror Image and Blur on the name: from the effect tags; Mirror Image shows one copy per duplicate left as you count them down.
- Magical Secrets: the picks are among the spells known, so the "Spells known" list asks for that many fewer; swapping a Magical Secrets spell on level-up is a new pick.
- Piercer: after the damage, one die is rolled again (tap it; with your own dice, type what it showed and shows now). Once per turn is yours to keep. With your own dice entered as one total the reroll line adds the difference.
- Critical range by hand (Sheet → Critical hit): every attack roll; a feature that gives more still counts.
- Beacon of Hope with 3D dice: each die lands, then turns to its maximum. A d4 turns only in a theme that reads it face-down; d100 doesn't turn (the value still counts as its maximum).
- A spell already on a companion or summoned creature can't be put on it again from + Effect or when casting; change its level or end it on its card.
- Peerless Skill and Homing Strikes: ticking spends the use when the roll is made. Homing Strikes' die is spent only if the attack then hits: if it still misses, give the die back on the Play page.
- Countercharm: while it's on, every save asks whether it's against being charmed or frightened (tick for advantage); friends within 30 ft are theirs to apply.
- Primeval Awareness: the ranger spell slot is marked by hand; what it senses is the DM's.
- Hide in Plain Sight: +10 Stealth while switched on; switch it off when you move, act or react.
- Hex Warrior: an option on every weapon you're proficient with that isn't two-handed (any weapon with Pact of the Blade); which one is your hex weapon this long rest is yours to keep.
- Elven Accuracy: three d20s with advantage on attacks that don't use Strength (weapon attacks by their ability, spell attacks, a Strength weapon with Shillelagh or Hex Warrior ticked).
- Piercer: the extra critical die on piercing weapons is rolled; the reroll of one die is offered after the damage. Not on piercing spells.
- Flames of Phlegethos: 1s are rerolled once on spells whose main damage is fire; the flame wreath is its own action.
- Gift of the Ever-Living Ones: switch "Familiar within 100 ft" on; then your Hit Dice, your own healing features and your healing spells on yourself are the maximum. Healing from others isn't affected.

## Spells (round 13d)

- Book spells' mechanics apply when the spell itself comes from your book files (the app has no text for them); their tags exist either way.
- Booming Blade and Green-Flame Blade: cast the spell, then make the melee weapon attack from Actions and tick its rider. The turn tracker may count the attack as the Attack action's: it's part of the spell (no Extra Attack).
- Time Stop: the turns are rolled and counted on its tag; whether something you did ends it early is yours to judge.
- Arcane Hand: the hand's own hit points and AC are on its tag, not tracked. Forceful and Interposing Hand are explained, not rolled.
- Animate Objects: pick up to the number of objects; a Medium object counts as two, a Large as four, a Huge as eight (the picker doesn't weigh them).
- Holy Weapon, Elemental Weapon, Spirit Shroud, Zephyr Strike: offered on every weapon roll; which weapon or target it applies to is yours to tick.
- Blade Ward and the Investiture of Stone's resistance: halve weapon damage by hand (spells aren't halved).
- Tenser's Transformation and Otherworldly Guise: the proficiencies and the spellcasting ability on weapon attacks are reminders.
- Lightning Arrow: roll the attack with the weapon, then the bolt instead of its damage.
- Antimagic Field: your own magic being suppressed inside it isn't switched off by the app.
- Conjure Barrage and Conjure Volley: pick your weapon's damage type when casting, then roll the dice from the tag once per creature.
- Wish: a duplicated spell is on your Spells tab while its tag is on (end the tag after casting). Anything else is your DM's; the stress is a tag: Strength 3 for the rolled days (resting days that take 2 off are counted by hand), the d100, and the necrotic d10s per spell level you roll from the tag.
- Simulacrum: from the bestiary (beasts and humanoids). A copy of a character is made as a companion of your own, at half its hit points.

## Magic items (round 13e)

- Casting from a wand or staff spends its charges by the level picked; a charge used any other way (Staff of Power's extra 1d6 force) is tapped on the item.
- Frost Brand, Holy Avenger, Dragon Slayer, Giant Slayer and Vorpal Sword: pick which sword (or axe) on the item; a longsword until then.
- Winged Boots: the 4 hours a day are yours to count.
- Ring of Spell Storing and Ioun Stone of Reserve: what they hold goes in the item's notes.
- Bag of Holding: its 500 lb capacity isn't checked.

## College of Spirits and Way of the Ascendant Dragon (round 13f)

- Tales from Beyond: the tale you hold is a tag until you bestow it or rest; its effect on the target is applied by you or the DM.
- Spiritual Focus: the d6 is offered on your bard spells' damage and healing rolls; that it went through the focus, and one roll per spell, are yours.
- Spirit Session: pick this time's spell in Choices; you know it while the session's tag is on (until your long rest). Its level against how many took part is yours to check.
- Draconic Presence: spend its use only when the reroll succeeds.
- Breath of the Dragon: it takes one attack of the Attack action; the augmented breath (17th) spends its own ki and the breath's use separately.
- Aspect of the Wyrm: Frightful Presence's saves are rolled by the DM; the resistance is yours (allies in the aura apply it themselves).

## Races (round 13f)

- Genasi, Duergar, Sea Elf, Shadar-kai, Deep Gnome: abilities, senses, resistances, speeds and innate spells are applied; Unending Breath, Earth Walk, Amphibious, Friend of the Sea and the sunlight limit on Duergar Magic are reminders.
- Shadar-kai: after the teleport, switch on "ghostly" for the resistance (from 3rd level); it ends at the start of your next turn.

## Falling, breath, underwater, surprise (round 13g)

- Falling: the height is yours to say; landing in water or other softer ground is the DM's.
- Holding your breath: start it from Actions; end its tag when you breathe again. Suffocating's "can't regain hit points or be stabilized" is a reminder.
- Underwater: add the Underwater tag from + Effect (Other); being only partly in water (no fire resistance) is yours to judge.
- Surprised: the turn tracker doesn't stop you acting on that first turn; the tag reminds you.

## Play page uses (round 14a)

- A use that casts a spell for free (Mystic Arcanum, Signature Spells, Misty Wanderer, Star Map, Walker in Dreams, Telekinetic Master, Shapechanger, Primal Awareness) opens that spell. Before its spell is picked in Choices, or when the spell is in a book file that isn't loaded (Summon Fey, Evard's Black Tentacles), the panel says so and the use can still be spent by hand.
- An option ticked on a roll (Dark One's Own Luck) says where it's ticked; "Spend one anyway" spends it without a roll.
- Relentless Rage and Overchannel are counts the app keeps itself; a tap explains them and can add one by hand.
- Draconic Presence: its use is spent only by "It worked: spend the use".

## Monk and cleric features (round 14b)

- Physician's Touch: a free action after Hand of Healing ends the condition you pick on you; on someone else, and diseases, it's theirs to remove. Hand of Harm's poisoned rider is a note.
- Flurry of Healing and Harm was already in the Flurry panel (Hand of Healing in place of strikes, Hand of Harm with no ki from 11th).
- Tranquility: Sanctuary is put on you at the end of each long rest, until the next one; its other ways of ending are yours to apply (end the tag).
- Intoxicated Frenzy: the Flurry asks for two to five strikes; that each one targets a different creature is yours to keep.
- Keeper of Souls: type the creature's number of Hit Dice; once per round and the kind of creature are yours to check.
- Expansive Bond: Protective Bond puts a resistance tag on you that halves the next damage you enter (any type) and then ends; if you enter none it lasts one round. The 60 ft range is in the notes; Emboldening Bond's range is yours to keep.
- Voice of Authority: a reminder in the cast's note for every spell of 1st level or higher; whether it targeted an ally is yours to judge.

## Round 14c

- Lines on other actions' notes (what the feature changes about them): Arcane Charge on Action Surge, Instinctive Pounce on Rage, Hold the Line and Vigilant Defender on Opportunity Attack, Creative Crescendo on Performance of Creation, Bestial Fury on Command companion.
- Size: your size now is on the Play page (Giant's Might, Runic Juggernaut's Huge, Enlarge/Reduce) and counts for carrying. Reach from Runic Juggernaut shows on melee attacks; other reach changes aren't tracked.

- Guardian Spirit: Spirit Totem's tag from 10th level, with the healing your summons get; applying it to them is yours.

## Round 14d

- Counterspell and Dispel Magic: after casting (or from the spell), pick the level of the spell you're up against; above your slot, the check is rolled with your spellcasting ability against DC 10 + its level. Improved Abjuration's proficiency bonus is in it. Whether it worked is the DM's to say.
- Heroes' Feast on you: roll the 2d10 (or pick what your dice showed); the maximum and current hit points go up, and the tag gives the immunities and advantage on Wisdom saves for 24 hours. Others at the feast apply it on their own sheets.
- Goodberry: ten berries on a tag for 24 hours; "Eat a berry" heals 1 and counts one off. Berries you give away: take them off with −.
- Lesser and Greater Restoration on you: the condition you pick (or one exhaustion level) ends; nothing stays as a tag. A curse or a reduced score or maximum is yours to undo.
- A casting that takes minutes ("Cast it now"): fixed in 14d; it used to stop after starting.

## Damage types, Transmuted Spell and the storm (round 15a)

- Options for some damage types only (Destructive Wrath: lightning or thunder; Radiant Soul: radiant or fire; Elemental Affinity: your dragon's type) are offered when the roll has dice of that type, after Transmuted Spell or any type you pick. Destructive Wrath maxes only those dice (on a weapon, Divine Strike's thunder, not the mace's).
- Destructive Wrath on a roll made elsewhere (a reaction from the DM's turn): its action still spends the use by hand.
- Thunderbolt Strike is a reminder on lightning damage rolls; the push is yours.
- Heart of the Storm and Tempestuous Magic are reminders when you cast; the burst's damage is its own action (Heart of the Storm burst), rolled by you.
- Empowered Spell: after a spell's damage roll, pick up to your Charisma modifier dice to roll again (1 sorcery point). With your own dice, enter what they showed and show now, in all.
- Circle of Mortality: "maximum healing" on healing rolls; whether the creature is at 0 hit points is yours to tick.
