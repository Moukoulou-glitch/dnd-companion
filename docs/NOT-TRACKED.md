# Not tracked yet

Things the app shows as a description only, or tracks partly. Kept up to date as rounds land; strike items when they become tracked.

## Engine limits (affect several features)
- **Speeds that depend on a choice after a rest** (Path of the Beast's Bestial Soul) or on a form (Circle of Stars' Dragon) aren't tracked; other fly, swim and climb speeds from features and spells (Fly, Spider Climb, Gaseous Form) are.
- **Feature timers** come from the duration a use states ("for 1 minute"); a feature whose wording doesn't say it gets none.

## Per class (summary only, or partly tracked)
- **Barbarian**: Totem Warrior Wolf spirit/aspect, Bear and Eagle attunement; Shielding Storm; Warrior of the Gods; Rage Beyond Death; Controlled Surge; Instinctive Pounce.
- **Bard**: Combat Inspiration, Mote of Potential, Unfailing Inspiration, Creative Crescendo, Blade Flourish +10 ft speed; Magical Inspiration and Bardic Versatility (summary).
- **Cleric**: Blessed Strikes, Improved Flare, Thunderbolt Strike, Stormborn, Improved Duplicity, Voice of Authority, Order's Wrath, Keeper of Souls, Expansive Bond, Circle of Mortality's maximized healing; Several conditional bonuses are tick-boxes, not automatic (Soul of the Forge, Avatar of Battle, Saint of Forge and Fire).
- **Druid**: Moon druid features rely on the app's Wild Shape handling; Mighty Summoner, Guardian Spirit, Twinkling Constellations, Fungal Body, Primal Strike.
- **Fighter**: Arcane Shot effects and their 18th-level increase; Rune Knight's individual rune passives (one shared counter); Cavalier/Samurai "or a language" alternatives; Samurai's alternate save; Know Your Enemy, Relentless, Weapon Bond's no-disarm, Arcane Charge, Hold the Line, Vigilant Defender, Rapid Strike, Great Stature, Master of Runes, Runic Juggernaut; Martial Versatility.
- **Monk**: Four Elements discipline effects; Astral Self's Wisdom-for-attacks and three-attack rule; Kensei's one-melee-one-ranged rule; Intoxicated Frenzy; Dedicated Weapon.
- **Paladin**: Aura of Conquest and Scornful Rebuke (damage to others), Emissary of Redemption, Invincible Conqueror's extra attack (its resistances and crit range are tracked), Mortal Bulwark's truesight, Living Legend/Mortal Bulwark slot refuel, Aura of Alacrity and Aura of the Sentinel (only your own bonus).
- **Ranger**: Mighty Swarm (built into Gathered Swarm's die).
- **Rogue**: Infiltration Expertise, Impostor, Magical Ambush, Insightful Manipulator, Soul of Deceit, Fancy Footwork, Death's Friend; Whispers of the Dead offers skills only (no tools).
- **Sorcerer**: Controlled Chaos, Unearthly Recovery's half-HP heal; Additional Sorcerer Spells, Sorcerous Versatility.
- **Warlock**: Gift of the Sea swim speed, Elemental Gift flight and its genie-kind resistance, Awakened Mind telepathy, Beguiling Defenses' charm immunity, Hex Warrior's Charisma for weapon attacks, Genie's Wrath damage type.
- **Wizard**: Arcane Ward is tracked as full after each long rest even before it's created; Savant features, Minor Alchemy, Focused Conjuration, Durable Summons, Split Enchantment, Alter Memories, Malleable Illusions, Master of Hexes, Sanctuary Vessel, Master Scrivener; Transmuter's Stone benefit; Grim Harvest healing (note); Durable Magic uses a switch you turn on while concentrating.

## Feats
- Maneuvers: the app adds the die and spends it; what the maneuver does to the target (prone, pushed, frightened) is text with its DC. Sweeping Attack's second target, Commander's Strike's ally attack, Evasive Footwork's AC while moving and Parry's reduction are shown, not applied.
- One maneuver per attack is a warning, not a block.
- Smites: the next-hit tag ends when its damage is rolled (Thunderous Smite's concentration too; the others keep concentration for their lasting part); the spell's lasting part (Branding's light, Blinding's blindness, Staggering's save) is text.
- Elemental Adept's minimum per die is applied when the app rolls; with your own dice, count low dice up yourself (the roll says so).
- Healer's once-per-creature limit resets when you rest (the app doesn't know when others rest).
- Ritual Caster's rituals can be cast any way the app allows; cast them as rituals.
- Heavy Armor Master needs you to mark magical damage (the switch shows on the damage pad when it applies).

## Your own actions and spells
- A custom attack uses the Attack action or a bonus action; a reaction attack is a "use" with its note, rolled from the Actions list like any attack of that kind.
- A custom spell needs a spell list to cast with; a character with no spellcasting gets no attack bonus or DC for it.
- Conditions a custom action imposes are named in its note; put them on the target yourself.

## Races (Volo's)
- Triton's Wall of Water isn't in the spell content (it's an XGtE spell); cast it from the book.
- Long-Limbed reach, Powerful Build, Surprise Attack's once per combat and Feline Agility's recharge are reminders, not tracked.

## Backstory and calendars
- The Μηθειολόγιο copy has the months, week, seasons and era from the table's Fantasy Calendar; moons, events and named years aren't shown, and the app doesn't sync with the website (set the date by hand if it moves on there).

## Auras and tags
- Aura effects from allies (Add an effect → Other) don't know whether you're inside the aura or whether its owner is conscious: remove them yourself.
- Twilight Sanctuary's per-turn temporary hit points aren't rolled for other creatures; the reminder says what to do.
- Feature DCs are listed for the features that need one (Stunning Strike, Turn Undead, maneuvers, Breath Weapon, ...); a feature missing from that list shows no DC.

## Summoned creatures and forms
- Grappled/restrained speeds etc. come from effects only; "lethargy"-style effects on summons aren't automatic.
- Creature spell slots for creatures without a Spellcasting trait are free-form ("cast anyway").

## Other
- Barbarian override in the table pack only covers levels up to 9 explicitly (higher levels come from the SRD).
- Weapon-specific Shillelagh: offered on every melee weapon, flagged when it isn't a club or quarterstaff.

## Effects
- Effects are drawn where you last tapped (spells, Inspiration) or at the roll's total / HP box / concentration chip; there's no per-spell-row anchor.
- Damage prevention shows for immunity, resistance, Heavy Armor Master, temp HP and Durable; a reduction rolled by hand before entering the damage (Parry, Deflect Missiles' panel) is already subtracted and doesn't show a shield.
- No sound. Vibration on Android only.
- 3D dice: only rolls made in the roll panel are thrown; quick rolls the app makes by itself (a feature's temp HP, starting gold) stay digital. A d3 or d100 in a roll is rolled by the app. The default dice show 6 and 9 alike when upside down. Reduced motion doesn't turn 3D dice off (it's your explicit choice).
- Damage types: a roll with several types plays only the main one (the most damage). Typed damage you take plays only when all of it got through; when some was stopped, the shield ring plays. Necrotic Shroud's frightening and Form of Dread's frightening hit are on other creatures, so they have no effect here.
- Nature spells: where the area is isn't recorded, so the looks grow toward where you tapped. Grasping Vine's later lashes (bonus action, no damage) have no effect; Plant Growth and Wall of Thorns have no look of their own yet (the arcane circle plays).
- Spell effects: Moonbeam's position and movement and Gust of Wind's direction aren't recorded, so their effects can't follow them. Control Water's or Weather's mode changed later (a new action) isn't recorded: change the tag's choice by hand. Hand of Harm in a Flurry at 11th (no ki) shows no effect.

## Spells with two damage types
- Flame Strike: casting it higher adds the extra 1d6 to the fire; the spell lets you put it on the radiant instead (add it by hand if you do).
- Spell lists exported one class at a time give their class only when the file name says whose list it is ("spells_available_to_druids…"); a renamed file loses that.

## Items and scrolls
- Spell scrolls: the optional Scroll Mishap table is text only. A spell you write yourself for a scroll also joins your own spells.
- Spells given beyond the rules can be cast without a slot as often as you like: the app counts, the DM decides how many.

## Combat menus
- War Caster knows which spells can target only one creature for the SRD spells and the table's placeholders; a spell that exists only in your book file isn't offered (cast it from the Spells tab).
- Long-press was checked in a desktop browser with mouse and simulated touch; not yet on a real iPhone or Android phone.
- Healing Hands on someone else: the app spends the use and says how much; the other character's HP is changed on their own sheet.

## Expanded lists and custom effects
- Tasha's expanded lists are names-only data in the table pack; a spell the app doesn't have yet (not SRD, not in your book files) can't appear until a book file adds it. The table's ranger has no separate “Additional Ranger Spells” switch. Primal Awareness's spells are free once per long rest each; casting them with a slot works as usual.
- A custom spell's effect is applied when it's on you (cast with “Put its effect on me”); on another creature it's a reminder for them.
- The custom effect's description and a custom spell's reminder are shown, not applied.

## Custom items and notes
- Your own items can't be weapons or armor (no attack, no armor class from wearing it): for an attack, make your own action; for AC, add an Armor Class change to the item.
- An item's description is shown, not applied; only the changes you add are.
- Markdown: no tables, images or underline in the Backstory and notes; a list inside a list needs two spaces in front.
- A note written before in-world dates were kept is placed by its date text; if the table changed calendars since, it goes under “No in-world date” (its date text still shows).

## Your own companions
- One stat block each (no forms to switch between). Skills, damage resistances and condition immunities from a stat block go in its traits as text; checks use the ability modifier.
- Your own companions don't get command buttons (unless bonded as your Ranger's Companion): they act on their own turn or as your DM says. Spending your action or bonus action to command one is up to you (the combat pills).
- Ranger's Companion (bonded): the proficiency bonus goes on its AC, attacks, damage and the saves its stat block lists; its skill proficiencies aren't tracked (checks use the ability modifier). From 5th level, commanding it to attack and still making one weapon attack yourself is up to you; Exceptional Training's bonus-action command, Bestial Fury's second attack and Share Spells are text. A new beast after 8 hours: change the stat block in the builder. Only one should be bonded; the sheet warns if more are.
- An attack's note (a save, knocking prone) is shown, not applied.

## Ranger (Tasha's or PHB)
- Deft Explorer: Tireless's temporary hit points have no minimum of 1 applied (only with a negative Wisdom modifier would it matter); removing exhaustion on a short rest is text. Nature's Veil switches a "Nature's Veil" state on until the end of your next turn; being invisible is up to you (add the Invisible condition if your table wants its effects).
- Martial Versatility and Cantrip/Fighting Style swaps at an Ability Score Improvement: change the pick on the Sheet.
- Natural Explorer and Favored Enemy (PHB) keep their SRD text; their benefits (terrain travel, tracking, languages) aren't applied as numbers.

## Drakewarden
- The drake's Hit Dice (d10s equal to your ranger level) aren't tracked; summoning it again brings it back at full hit points.
- Infused Strikes (its reaction: +1d6 of its essence on an ally's weapon hit) and Drake Mount (riding it, no flying while ridden until 15th) are shown as traits, not rolled or applied.
- Drake's Breath rolls its damage when used and shows your spell save DC; the type you pick is named on the use, and the targets' saves are up to the table. Spending a 3rd-level or higher slot for another use: spend it on the slot pills.
- Reflexive Resistance counts its uses; halving the damage is up to you (or apply it as resistance when you enter the damage).
- Your resistance to the drake's essence lasts while it's summoned with hit points left; if it's dismissed some other way, summoning state isn't tracked beyond its hit points.
